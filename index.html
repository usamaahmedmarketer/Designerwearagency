How I Work

From moodboard to sold out

A collection goes through moodboard, pattern, sample, and production before it hits the rack. Your ad account goes through the same five stages.

Stage 01Moodboard

Market & Audience Read

Competitor ad libraries, price-tier positioning, and audience profiling before a single rupee is spent. Designer wear ads live and die on whether the offer matches who's actually looking.

Stage 02Pattern

Funnel Architecture

The full customer journey gets cut like a pattern — TOF discovery, MOF consideration, BOF conversion — so nothing leaks between "loved it" and "bought it."

Stage 03Sample

Launch & Creative Testing

Tracking goes in first. Then small-batch creative testing — different silhouettes of the same message — before real budget touches any single ad.

Stage 04Production

Data-Driven Scaling

Budget follows the data, not the favourite. Winning creative and audiences get the production run; everything else gets cut fast.

Stage 05Alterations

Retention & Aftercare

Klaviyo flows, retargeting sequences, and repeat-purchase nurture — the after-sales service that turns a one-time buyer into a return customer for the next drop.

The Fabric of This Niche

What's actually different about selling at 60K–150K PKR

Designer wear doesn't buy like fast fashion, and it doesn't buy like mass e-commerce either. This is what I build around.

Ad Fatigue Is the Real Enemy

Every designer wear account runs the same try-on reel. I build creative variance into the testing plan from day one so the account isn't fighting its own category for attention.

Longer Consideration Windows

A 100K PKR outfit isn't an impulse buy. Retargeting windows, frequency caps, and email nurture are set for a multi-week decision, not a same-day one.

Season Is the Media Plan

Eid, wedding season, and formal drop calendars decide budget pacing months in advance — not the week the collection actually launches.

Catalog Built for Variants

Size, colourway, and unstitched-vs-stitched SKUs need a catalog structure that doesn't fall apart the moment DPA tries to retarget the wrong variant.

Price-Tier Psychology

Ad copy and offer structure change depending on whether the buyer is comparing you to ready-to-wear or to a tailor — that framing shift is most of the conversion rate.

Bridal Is Its Own Funnel

Bridal buyers research for months before they message. WhatsApp and email nurture sequences matter as much as the ad that started the conversation.

Before You Book

Questions every brand owner asks

I already have someone running my ads — why switch?

Most media buyers split their attention across every niche that walks in. I only run designer wear and occasion wear — the fatigue patterns, the price-tier psychology, the seasonal calendar. You get someone who's already made the expensive mistakes on someone else's budget, not yours.

How fast will I actually see a difference?

Tracking and audit fixes show up in the first 2–3 weeks. Real scale takes longer here than in fast fashion — a 100K PKR outfit has a multi-week consideration window, so I'm usually looking at 60–90 days for the full funnel to compound.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. It's a month-to-month retainer. If the account isn't performing, you're not locked into six months of it.

Do you create the content too, or just manage the ads?

Raw content editing and creative testing are built into every tier. Full production — shoots, styling — isn't, but I'll work directly with your photographer or videographer to brief what the ad account actually needs.

What if my sales are inconsistent right now — is it too early to scale?

That's exactly what the audit and research phase is for. Tracking, offer, and creative get fixed before more budget goes anywhere near the account. Scaling an unfixed leak just makes the leak bigger.

Do you work with brands outside Pakistan?

Yes — currently managing accounts across Pakistan, UAE, UK, USA, and Saudi Arabia. It's a remote-first setup: WhatsApp for daily updates, a monthly reporting call, and full visibility into the ad accounts at all times.

What's the minimum ad budget I need to work with you?

Enough to generate statistically meaningful testing data. For most designer wear brands that's around 150K+ PKR/month in media spend — separate from the retainer itself.
